A Letter
September 2026  ·  Thiruvalla

To my Kassel Batch, with all my heart —

ചില ക്ലാസുകൾ അവസാനിച്ചാലും നമ്മുടെ മനസ്സിൽ നിന്ന് ഒരിക്കലും അവസാനിക്കില്ല. എനിക്ക് അത്തരത്തിലുള്ള ഒരു ക്ലാസാണ് Kassel Batch.

When I walked into that classroom for the first time, I carried lesson plans, grammar notes, and vocabulary lists. I did not know I would leave with something far more important — a memory I will carry for the rest of my life.

You were not the easiest batch. You were not the quietest. But you were present. Every class, every repeated sentence, every correction that was heard — that presence made this classroom alive.

Naveen, Nandhana, Varsha, Justin, Anjitha, Sandra Sebastian, Kripa, Sandra Sabu, Anu, Jeffry, Aleena — നിങ്ങൾ ഓരോരുത്തരും ഈ classroom-ൽ ഒരു വ്യത്യാസം ഉണ്ടാക്കി. ഒരുപക്ഷേ നിങ്ങൾക്ക് അത് അറിയില്ലായിരിക്കും.

You came to learn German. But in the process, you taught me something about patience, about the courage it takes to speak a foreign language for the first time, and about what it means to sit inside a classroom with a dream already burning inside you.

„Der Weg ist das Ziel.“ — The journey is the destination. German was never just an exam to pass. It was a bridge. And you built that bridge, one word at a time.

ഇനി ഒരു നല്ല ഭാവി ഉണ്ടാകട്ടെ. Germany-ൽ എത്തുമ്പോൾ, ആ ആദ്യ Guten Morgen പറയുമ്പോൾ — ഓർക്കൂ, ആ വാക്ക് ഈ classroom-ൽ നിന്ന് ആരംഭിച്ചതാണ്.

Go. Dream. Learn. Stumble. Get back up. And keep going. Immer weiter. Always forward.

Clifford Your Deutschlehrer

“I taught you German.

Every batch teaches a teacher something. You taught me that progress is not always a big achievement. Sometimes, it is simply the courage to speak one more sentence.
